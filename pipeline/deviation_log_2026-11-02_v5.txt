0. RULES (decided 2026-11-02, C.S.)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Registration, every later session:
  1. FEAT's standard registration (each run -> first-session T1, BBR) by default.
  2. If FEAT's runs disagree with each other by more than 5 mm, run step 07
     (each run -> first-session run-02 functional -> first-session FEAT transform).
  3. Keep step 07 only if its runs then agree within ~1 mm (validated range
     0.06-0.30 mm on 092/108). Otherwise keep FEAT and flag the session as a
     registration failure.
  Why 5 mm: FEAT's failures were 12-86 mm; every other session with a FEAT value
  was <= 2.2 mm (longitudinal_qc_compiled.csv, 39 sessions), so any threshold in
  that gap selects the same sessions. A 1 mm threshold would also catch sessions
  FEAT handled acceptably (sub-004 ses-02 1.5 mm, ses-03 2.2 mm; sub-018 ses-02
  1.1 mm, a first session, which has no step-07 fallback).
  First sessions have no fallback: a FEAT failure there is flagged.

Every session FEAT failed, and the outcome:
  session          FEAT runs disagree (max)   step 07 runs agree    outcome
  sub-092 ses-02   85.8 mm                    0.06-0.30 mm (both)   step 07 (10-31)
  sub-108 ses-02   38.6 mm                    0.06-0.30 mm (both)   step 07 (10-31)
  sub-010 ses-03   12.1 mm                    0.09-0.31 mm          step 07 (11-02)
  sub-008 ses-02   22.4 mm                    0.33-0.84 mm          step 07 (11-02)
  sub-004 ses-05   28.0 mm                    up to 297 mm          FEAT kept, FLAGGED

Acquisition (C.S., 2026-11-02): 27-slice sessions (27 x 2.5 mm, old Verio slab

from whole-brain and connectivity analyses.

only sub-004 and sub-021 qualify, so they are dropped.

2. WHY (as v3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEAT's direct registration was inconsistent across runs of the same session
(run-to-run rmsdiff, after accounting for measured head movement between runs):
  sub-092 ses-02   13.8 - 85.8 mm
  sub-108 ses-02   20.2 - 38.6 mm
  good sessions    0.1 - 0.3 mm (sub-079 ses-02; ses-01 of 079/092/108: 0.15-0.33)
sub-092 run-01 vs run-02 head movement was under 0.5 deg / 0.3 mm, yet FEAT
placed run-01 about 60 deg and 68/89 mm away from runs 02 and 03.

Ruled out as causes:
  - pipeline setup: sub-diff (copy of sub-079) reproduced 079 exactly; 1stLevel
    .fsf identical between ses-01 and ses-02 apart from the anatomy path
  - anatomy FEAT used: reg/highres and highres_head identical (maxdiff 0)
  - scan protocol: JSON identical except converter version (sub-092 ses-02:
    dcm2niix v1.0.20260416 vs v1.0.20201102 elsewhere)
  - headers and voxel values: same structure, ordinary session-to-session range
  - head position between sessions: ses-02 vs ses-01 functional within ~2 deg,
    <8 mm; functional-to-functional correlation 0.98-0.99
  - T1 identity: ses-02 vs ses-01 T1 correlation 0.95-0.96 (079: 0.99)
  - timing: events match the .mat stimulus order, run matching, labels and the
    8.0 s start offset for both subjects; matches old timing files within 16 ms
  - (v4) slice count: of the three later FEAT failures, only sub-004 ses-05 is
    27-slice; sub-010 ses-03 is 69 x 2 mm in both sessions.
Cause of the FEAT failure: not identified [inferred: fragile direct
cross-session alignment].

Precedent:
  - SPM's multi-session realignment aligns each session's first scan to the
    first session's first scan, then coregisters once to anatomy (SPM manual).
    SPM "sessions" are usually runs within one visit.
  - (v4) Bonelli et al. 2012, Epilepsia (doi 10.1111/j.1528-1167.2012.03433.x),
    longitudinal fMRI before and ~4 months after anterior temporal lobe
    resection in adults: postoperative scans realigned, coregistered to the
    preoperative mean image, and normalized with the preoperative parameters.
    Closest published precedent found. Differences: Bonelli aligned one mean
    image per session; step 07 aligns each run's example_func. Adults, months
    apart, not developing brains across years.
  Citation choice for the methods: still to settle.
